As a middle-aged man who works in tech, it has been a matter of some consternation that I have never appeared on a podcast. Well, this terrible state of affairs has now come to an end.

Here’s the blurb:

> Had a great time recording this episode of The Principal Podcast with Dennis Black, where I broke down what I’ve learned helping customers get more value from GridGain[^gridgain], solve complex technical problems, and build the knowledge to become more self-sufficient.
> 
> My career into consulting was never really planned. It started almost by accident, but over time I realized I was most energized by working directly with customers, understanding how they use technology, and helping them solve problems that have a real business impact.
> 
> Here are three lessons I shared from that journey:
>
> * The best consulting does more than fix a problem: it explains the how and why so the customer can handle more on their own next time.
> * In enterprise technology, technical value is only part of the equation: you also have to navigate stakeholders, compliance, and the realities of operating at scale.
> * Growth often starts with awareness: there are still many Apache Ignite users who could benefit from GridGain but simply do not know the connection exists.
>
> Hosted by Dennis Black, this conversation goes into product consulting, customer education, open source, enterprise sales, and the opportunities ahead for GridGain.

[^gridgain]: I don’t typically talk of the specifics of my work here, but I think I need to provide a little context. Until recently, I worked for a company called GridGain, who were behind the Open Source database [Apache Ignite](https://ignite.apache.org). Recently they were bought by MariaDB, another database company. And that’s where I work now.
